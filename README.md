# Boeing Financial Statement Analysis

A team-based financial statement analysis of Boeing Co., benchmarking against Lockheed Martin and Raytheon, with a focus on profitability, asset utilization, liquidity, solvency, and valuation.

---

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a group assignment focused on financial statement analysis. Using Boeing’s annual filings and financial ratios, we analyzed the company’s financial health and strategic position within the aerospace and defense industry. We also compared key metrics against two major competitors: Lockheed Martin and Raytheon Technologies.

## Key Areas Covered

- DuPont ROE and ROA Disaggregation  
- Asset recognition, turnover ratios, and percent used-up  
- Inventory and depreciation method comparison  
- Liquidity and solvency ratio analysis  
- Equity valuation context despite negative net income  
- Credit rating discussion and 2024 outlook  

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel (full workbook included)  
- Public 10-K filings from Boeing, Lockheed Martin, and Raytheon  
- Instructor-guided FSA framework  

---

## Summary Insights

- Boeing reported negative net income and negative equity, leading to inconclusive ROE/ROA using DuPont analysis  
- Boeing faces higher solvency and liquidity risk compared to Lockheed and Raytheon  
- Despite these risks, Boeing remains an index giant due to market dominance, long-term government contracts, and economies of scale
